RELIABILITY METRICS

Challenges & Mitigations

AI Hallucinations

**Challenge**: Model misidentifying shadows or carvings as material fissures.

**Mitigation**: Sorting predictions into trust levels: Documented (100%), Inferred (89%), Uncertain (64%).

Compute Burden

**Challenge**: Rebuilding dense 3D meshes daily requires massive cloud cycles.

**Mitigation**: Implementing incremental reconstruction, matching keyframes directly to baseline twins.

Variable Lighting

**Challenge**: Changing shadows disrupt texture segmentation across seasons.

**Mitigation**: Applying photometric normalization and texture color correction before model sweeps.
